How it's made

Deconstructed.
Calibrated. Rebuilt.

  • On iPhone 12 and newer, Sable records 10-bit HDR, which holds far more light and color than a normal phone video.
  • The picture is built in Sable's own code: tone and color, the glow film gives bright lights, real grain, and softening that takes the digital edge off.
  • What you see while you shoot is exactly what you get.
  • Your original clip is always kept, so you can change the look later.
  • Editing on a computer? Sable sends the clip with matching LUTs to DaVinci Resolve.
Support

Need a hand?

Send a message through the support form and you'll hear back from the person who made the app, usually within a day or two.

Which iPhones does Sable work on?
Any iPhone on iOS 17 or later, which means iPhone XS, XR and newer. iPhone 12 and newer record 10-bit HDR; older iPhones record SDR.
Where do my videos go?
They stay in Sable on your phone until you save them. Save the finished video to Photos in 4K or 1080p, and the original HDR clip too.
Can I change the look after I shoot?
Yes. Sable keeps your original clip untouched, so you can adjust or turn off the look on any clip at any time.
Does it cost anything?
Sable is free to use, with no ads and no account.
Something isn't working.
Use the support form above and say which iPhone you have and what happened.
