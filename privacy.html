Privacy policy

Sable doesn't collect any data. Your videos stay on your iPhone, and nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or anyone else.

What the app uses, and why

Camera and microphone: to record video and sound. Recording happens on your phone only.

Photos (add only): to save videos to your photo library when you choose to. Sable can't see or read your existing photos.

You can turn any of these off at any time in Settings → Sable.

What we don't do

Sable has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't use third-party code that collects data, and it doesn't connect to a server.

Sharing

Videos leave your phone only when you share or export them yourself, using iOS sharing. What happens to them then is up to the app or person you send them to.

Children

Sable doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does, with a new effective date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Use the support form. It runs on Google Forms, and what you send there, including the email you give, is used only to reply to you.
